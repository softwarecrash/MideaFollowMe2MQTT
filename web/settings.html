Infrared & Follow Me

ESP_IR_TR_V1.1 uses normal polarity on GPIO4. Inverted polarity can leave its IR LED continuously powered and must not be used on this board.

Standalone mode always uses a detected DS18B20. In network mode the MQTT temperature has priority; the local sensor takes over only after the MQTT temperature expires.

Energy
Wi-Fi power saving and Deep Sleep remain disabled for at least 10 minutes after a manual restart. Keeping a browser page open pauses this countdown. During Deep Sleep the device is offline; retained MQTT set topics and a GPIO16-to-RST wake connection are required.
